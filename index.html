Ongoing

Batch Quantile Stochastic Gradient Descent

Harvey Mudd College · Advisor: Dr. Jamie Haddock
Second reader: Dr. Anna Ma, UC Irvine

Developing batch qk-SGD, a method that filters each mini-batch by an empirical loss quantile and randomly subsamples the retained observations for gradient updates.

Methods & research questions
  • Studying when the filtered gradient estimator is unbiased for its intended target, distinguishing conditional sampling within a retained batch from unbiasedness for the full-data objective.
  • Investigating assumptions for convergence to a minimizer of the retained-sample loss, including how the retained set depends on the current iterate.
  • Implementing numerical experiments to connect empirical optimization behavior with ongoing theoretical analysis.

Measles Outbreak Prediction

Harvey Mudd College · Advisor: Dr. Susan Martonosi

Modeling outbreak occurrence and magnitude using an integrated 2025 dataset for 221 Texas counties, combining demographic, socioeconomic, health, vaccination-exemption, enrollment, and case-count variables.

Methods & findings
  • Compared logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, and gradient-boosted trees; designed Pareto-front selection to identify nondominated classifiers under competing precision and recall objectives.
  • Modeled raw case counts with negative binomial regression, LASSO and bidirectional stepwise selection, and selection-adjusted inference. Estimated a rate ratio of 1.64 per one-percentage-point increase in conscientious vaccine exemptions (p = 0.0015).
  • Used SHAP values to examine predictor contributions and k-means clustering to characterize county profiles.

Collaboration: Harvey Mudd, RIT, CSU Long Beach, and Claremont Graduate University. Supported by the Norman F. Sprague III Experiential Learning Fund.

Ecological Inference of Voting Behavior

Claremont McKenna College · Advisor: Dr. Evan T. Rosenman

Investigating subgroup voting preferences from Los Angeles precinct returns, with a focus on unobserved race-by-vote relationships and sparse, compositionally inconsistent demographic covariates.

Methods & findings
  • Built an R pipeline reconciling voter-file and election records, addressing precinct-ID mismatches, missingness, small precincts, and voter-count/tally inconsistencies. Used Bayesian Improved Surname Geocoding (BISG; wru) and precinct aggregation to estimate racial composition.
  • Compared semiparametric (seine), Bayesian, and ridge-based estimators through finite-sample sensitivity analyses and simulations with known subgroup preferences.
  • Separated real-data aggregate-fit checks from simulated subgroup-recovery tests because individual race-by-vote choices are unobserved.
  • Identified seine subgroup estimates outside [0, 1], highlighting the need to assess probability feasibility alongside aggregate fit.
Topology & networks

Aug – Dec 2025

Topological Bicycle-Network Analysis

Pitzer College · Advisor: Dr. Bahar Acu

Studied connectivity and route redundancy in Claremont’s bicycle network using traffic-thresholded graphs from Strava Metro and OpenStreetMap.

Methods & findings
  • Constructed nested graphs with preprocessing and multiscale filtration to study removal of low-traffic edges.
  • Combined persistent homology and Betti-number summaries with Laplacian spectra and spanning-tree counts.
  • Found that connectivity depends primarily on major routes shared with motor traffic, while substantial lower-traffic alternatives remain available, informing the project’s bicycle-planning objective.
Discrete geometry

May – Aug 2025

Symmetric Edge Polytopes

Harvey Mudd College · Advisor: Dr. Andrés R. Vindas Meléndez

Studied graph-generated lattice polytopes and their fixed-point polytopes under coordinate-permutation actions, connecting graph isomorphisms, symmetries, and lattice-relative volumes.

Contributions & preprint
  • Contributed to a proof that simple, connected graphs are isomorphic if and only if their symmetric edge polytopes are unitarily equivalent; characterized induced symmetries through coordinate permutations up to global sign.
  • Characterized fixed polytopes as linear images of symmetric edge polytopes of contracted graphs; derived volume formulas from permutation cycle lengths, including explicit formulas for complete graphs.

Partially funded by the Rose Hills Foundation. Read the 2025 preprint.
